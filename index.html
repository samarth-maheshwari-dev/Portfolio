Project Aion — Industrial Knowledge AI

Living operational brain for industrial plants · RAG + Knowledge Graph

ET AI Hackathon 2026 RAG · AI

Built for the Economic Times AI Hackathon 2026. Plant documents (manuals, SOPs, incident reports, scanned PDFs) go through OCR, get embedded into a FAISS index and linked in an SQLite knowledge graph of equipment and incidents. Engineers ask in plain English and get answers with citations from Groq-hosted LLMs, with local Ollama as fallback. FastAPI backend with 19 REST endpoints, React frontend.
