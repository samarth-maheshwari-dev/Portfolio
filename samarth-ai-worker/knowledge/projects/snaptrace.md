# Snaptrace AI

Snaptrace AI is an intelligent platform designed to help students easily find and claim lost items on campus. 

By leveraging AI, it categorizes, matches, and streamlines the lost-and-found process, saving time and preventing permanent loss of belongings.

This project highlights Samarth's focus on solving real-world, localized problems using applied AI technology in web platforms.
